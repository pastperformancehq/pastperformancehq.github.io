Notes

  • Quantum’s thirty-five percent for 1999 is the figure reported at the time (Bloomberg News and CNNfn, 28 April 2000). In later interviews Druckenmiller says forty-two percent net.
  • His 2015 talk at the Lost Tree Club survives only as an unattributed transcript, with no first-party recording. Three details rest on that transcript alone: the favorite shares at more than a hundred times earnings, given as the manager’s view (“as you see it”); the fifty and seven percent, credited to “a talk years later”; and the phone, credited to “that talk” and, on the day of the record, to “his own account”. His recorded interviews of 2022, 2024 and 2026 confirm that he sold in January, was tempted, and bought back at the top, but not those three details.
  • Federal Open Market Committee transcripts are released after five years, so what they record was private at the time.
